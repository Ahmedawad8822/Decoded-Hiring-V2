FOR TEAMS AND OPEN COHORTS

The Workshop

Four practical days. Twenty modules. One evidence-to-recommendation operating system adapted to your hiring reality.

DAY 01 · DIAGNOSE

Diagnose & define

Turn the vacancy request into a shared role definition, hiring strategy and evidence standard. Leave with: a role brief and an evidence standard.

DAY 02 · MAP

Map & engage

Build a realistic target universe, choose the right attraction approach and start stronger candidate conversations. Leave with: a target universe and engagement plan.

DAY 03 · ASSESS

Assess & recommend

Replace interview opinion with structured evidence and recommendations that can withstand challenge. Leave with: a scorecard and evidence-backed recommendation.

DAY 04 · APPLY AI

Apply AI responsibly

Design useful AI-assisted workflows with human review, clear guardrails and a practical 30-day plan. Leave with: an AI-assisted workflow and a 30-day action plan.
