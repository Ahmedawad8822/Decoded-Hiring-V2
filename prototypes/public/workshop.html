FOUR DAYS / TWENTY MODULES

The programme moves from clarity to implementation.

DAY 01 — Diagnose and define 5 modules · clarity before activity
Recruitment strategy, stakeholder discovery, role clarity, outcomes and evidence standards. Leave with: a role brief and an evidence standard.
DAY 02 — Map and engage 5 modules · market to conversation
Market reality, target-company universes, attraction choices, sourcing and candidate conversation. Leave with: a target universe and engagement plan.
DAY 03 — Assess and recommend 5 modules · evidence over instinct
Competency scorecards, ROCKET interviews, evidence synthesis, quality checks and decision calibration. Leave with: a scorecard and evidence-backed recommendation.
DAY 04 — Apply AI responsibly 5 modules · accountable leverage
Workflow selection, prompt engineering, human review, guardrails, operating principles and a practical 30-day plan. Leave with: an AI-assisted workflow and a 30-day action plan.
